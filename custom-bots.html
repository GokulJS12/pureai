Design Philosophy

Digital Twin First.

Every custom bot starts as a complete digital twin — simulated in physics-accurate environments before a single component is manufactured. This eliminates redesign cycles and guarantees first-build success.

  • Full physics simulation with real-world parameters
  • Iterative optimization with AI-driven design exploration
  • Safety validation in 1000+ simulated scenarios
  • ROS2-native software stack from day one
Fabrication

Built to Endure.

Military-grade materials. Aerospace tolerances. Every custom bot is engineered for the harshest conditions your operation demands — from -40°C Arctic deployments to 65°C desert operations.

  • -40°C to 65°C operational range
  • IP67+ weatherproofing standard
  • Modular hot-swappable components
  • 8–24 hour continuous runtime options
