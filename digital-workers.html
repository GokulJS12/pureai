01 · The Thesis

Work is being
rewritten.

The last decade gave us software that answered questions. This decade gives us software that finishes work.

A Digital Worker is not a chatbot, not a script, and not an assistant. It is a persistent, goal-driven agent with memory, tools, judgment and the authority to act inside your systems of record.

At pureAI, we believe the next trillion hours of knowledge work will be performed by a hybrid workforce — humans setting direction, agents executing the long, branching middle.

02 · Definition

Not a tool.
A colleague.

A pureAI Digital Worker is a software entity that owns an outcome. It reads context, plans across multiple steps, calls the right APIs, verifies the result and reports back — all without a human in the critical path for routine cases.

Where automation followed rules, agents follow intent. They reason about novel situations, recover from failure, request approval when stakes cross a threshold, and learn from every cycle.

Each worker has a clearly scoped role, a stable identity in your IAM directory, audit logs of every action, and a defined escalation path back to its human counterpart.

04 · The Orchestration Layer

One conductor.
Many specialists.

Real work isn't atomic — it's a relay across departments, systems and seniorities. pureAI's orchestrator routes each request through the right sequence of specialist agents, with humans gated only at decision points that warrant them.

A single mortgage application, for example, passes through six agents — verifier, credit analyst, policy validator, risk modeler, recommender, and finally a human underwriter — in roughly the time a colleague would take to read the file.

07 · Lifecycle

A worker's
day.

Every Digital Worker on the pureAI platform follows the same operating loop — observable, governable, replayable. Below: a single shift, from a fresh task in the queue to a verified outcome.

T+0s · Ingest
Pick up the task.

A goal arrives — from a queue, an event, an email, a calendar trigger, or a peer agent. The worker hydrates context from memory and the systems of record.

T+2s · Plan
Decompose into steps.

The reasoning layer drafts a multi-step plan, selects the right tools and APIs, and predicts where human approval will be required.

T+5s · Act
Execute against systems.

Reads documents, queries databases, calls services, writes to ledgers. Every action is signed, logged and reversible.

T+22s · Verify
Check its own work.

Runs evaluators against the result. If confidence is below threshold, the worker self-corrects or escalates — never silently approves.

T+30s · Report
Close the loop.

Writes the decision back, updates downstream agents, notifies the human counterpart, and stores the episode for future reasoning.
