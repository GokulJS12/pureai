Inquiry privacy notice

Notice version 1 · 6 November 2026

Pure AI uses your name, email address, optional phone number and message to review your inquiry and contact you about it. Submitting this form does not subscribe you to marketing.

Our hosting provider processes the request, Cloudflare Turnstile checks for automated abuse, and Resend processes the inquiry email for delivery to our team. These providers may process information outside your country. Turnstile may process browser and device information for security checks.

The form does not store your details in browser storage. The inquiry and your consent are included in the email sent to our team. Email and service records may remain in our business mailbox and service-provider systems; access and retention are managed by Pure AI and its providers.

Please do not submit passwords, bank or card details, identity documents or other sensitive information. A submission confirmation means the email service accepted the inquiry for delivery; it does not confirm that a team member has read it or guarantee a response time.

For questions about your information, or to request access, correction or deletion, contact gkai@pureaiglobal.com. You can also use this email instead of the form.
