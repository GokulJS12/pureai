How Nano Bots Work

From deployment to autonomous operation — a four-phase intelligence cycle.

01

Deployment

Nano bots are introduced via injection, aerosol, or direct application — activating upon environmental contact.

02

Navigation

Chemical gradient sensing and magnetic field guidance navigate swarms to precise target locations.

03

Execution

Swarm consensus triggers coordinated action — drug release, repair, or environmental remediation.

04

Dissolution

Post-mission, nano bots safely biodegrade or are magnetically extracted — zero residual footprint.
